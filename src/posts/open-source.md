---
title: Maintaining Open Source Without Burning Out
description: Sustainable practices for open source maintainers.
date: '2025-2-20'
categories:
  - general
published: true
---

Set boundaries early. A clear contributing guide and a bot that auto-closes
stale issues will save your inbox. You are not obligated to review every
drive-by PR.

Sponsorships help, but the real sustainability comes from building a community
of co-maintainers who can triage and merge when you step away.
